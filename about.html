Who we are

A people shaped by
truth and grace.

We are learning to know God personally, live from our identity in Christ, and serve the purpose He has placed within us.

Our doctrine

Jesus Christ is the way, the truth, and the life.

Our faith is anchored in the doctrine of Jesus Christ and expressed through a life of love, holiness, fellowship, and service.

We believe the Scriptures reveal God's eternal truth and invite every person into a living relationship with Jesus. We make room for honest questions, patient study, and the transforming work of the Holy Spirit.

What guides us

A faith with roots.

02

Fulfilling Purpose

In Christ, every life carries calling. We help people discover their identity and walk boldly in it.

Our pastor

A shepherd for the journey.

Our pastor serves this house with a heart for Scripture, people, and the quiet work of making disciples.

Through teaching, prayer, and personal care, our leadership is committed to building a church where people can encounter God and grow into maturity.
